Aloha, MeiLi

Your care stays personal. The busywork goes away.

We propose to build MAB its own business system: one place where every bride's journey, every document and every team schedule lives.

It keeps what makes MAB special, your sales team's personal attention and your artistry, and removes the copy-paste, chasing and calendar back-and-forth behind them. It runs on the same framework we built for Eco Lux Organix, set up entirely in MAB's name, so you own your data, your domain and your billing.

What we heard

Today, one email runs on its own. Everything else runs on your sales team.

How it works now

  • Website inquiries trigger one Flodesk welcome email. Planners often email or call instead, so leads live in different places.
  • Your sales team checks availability and scope by hand, then personalizes one of several templates for each bride.
  • Calls with you are arranged by text and email, and nothing from them is captured for later.
  • Proposals, the style service agreement and the questionnaire go out together. Signatures, retainers and balances are tracked by hand.
  • After booking, the 30-day and week-of messages depend on the sales team's timing, with no set thank-you or review step.

What you asked for

  • An intake form with discovery-call booking from real open times
  • A personalized welcome and a Bridal Vision for every bride
  • Recorded calls with AI notes that fill the Look Book and packages
  • A consultation, proposals, agreements and payments in one flow
  • Timed emails from Congratulations to Post-Event, email only for now
  • Google Meet and Zoom, team logins and shared scheduling
  • A foundation for a future MAB Academy

The bridal journey

Twelve caring steps, with her Look Book at the heart

It keeps every touchpoint MAB has today and adds the discovery call your website already promises.

  1. Before she books
  2. InquiryShe shares her details and books a 20-minute discovery call from your sales team's real open times. Planners can inquire for a bride.
  3. WelcomeYour Maui or outer-island welcome, personalized with her name, date and venue, plus her Bridal Vision.
  4. Discovery call with your sales teamRecorded with consent and summarized by AI. She uploads her dress and four inspiration photos.
  5. Her Look BookBuilt from the call notes and her photos, reviewed by you or your sales team before she sees it.
  6. Consultation with youYou walk her through her Look Book and the experience you recommend.
  7. Proposal and agreementOne link to review, sign the style service agreement and pay the 50% retainer.
  8. After she books
  9. BookedThe date locks only when she has signed and paid. She receives a congratulations email and her own portal.
  10. Halfway thereTrial booking, extension color match, questionnaire, and a first look at her timeline.
  11. 30 days outHer balance link and final details.
  12. Week ofRoom number, prep tips and arrival times. Your artists receive a day-of brief.
  13. Wedding dayEveryone knows where to be and when.
  14. MahaloA thank-you from you, a review request, and a place to share her professional photos.

How we'll build it

In the order a bride travels, one checkpoint at a time

Each phase goes live before the next begins, so MAB benefits early. Flodesk and your current templates stay in place until the new system has proven itself.

Phase 0 · Foundations

Accounts in MAB's name, your final packages and pricing, your agreement, approved email wording and brand assets loaded.

Checkpoint: you approve the package catalog and the agreement.

Phase 1 · Inquiry to discovery

Intake form, pipeline, welcome emails, date check, discovery booking and reminders, team logins and calendars, bride portal and Bridal Vision.

Checkpoint: your sales team handles every new inquiry in the system.

Phase 2 · Calls to booking

Call recording and AI notes, uploads, the Look Book builder, consultations, proposals, e-signature and the retainer payment.

Checkpoint: the first bride signs and pays inside the system.

Phase 3 · Booked to Mahalo

Halfway, 30-day, balance and week-of emails, artist assignments and day-of briefs, the post-wedding review flow and your dashboard.

Checkpoint: one full wedding runs start to finish with no manual email.

Phase 4 · MAB Academy

Courses and certification on the same login, shaped with you once Phase 3 is live.
